# Spider-Man (2002)

First movie in the Tobey Maguire trilogy. Directed by **Sam Raimi**, starring Tobey Maguire as Peter Parker and Willem Dafoe as Norman Osborn / Green Goblin.

## Contexts
- Peter gets bitten by a radioactive spider on a field trip
- Struggles with rent, school, and keeping his identity secret from Mary Jane
- Classic iconic scenes like the *upside-down kiss* and stopping the Green Goblin at the end

> With great power comes great responsibility.
